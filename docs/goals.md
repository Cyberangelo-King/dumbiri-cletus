# Website Goals Document
## Dumbiri Cletus — Personal Brand Website

---

## 1. Primary Goal

**Convert visitors into booked discovery calls or qualified leads within their first session.**

Everything on this site — design, copy, architecture, animations — exists to serve this goal. A beautiful site that generates no bookings is a failure. A less beautiful site that generates 20 calls a month is a success.

---

## 2. Business Goals

| Goal | How the Website Serves It | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Generate coaching clients (real estate) | Dedicated service page, results-language, booking CTA | Discovery call bookings from /services/real-estate-coaching |
| Fill realtor training cohorts | Training page with curriculum overview, spots-remaining urgency, group/individual options | Form submissions tagged "training" |
| Win brand design projects | Portfolio page with quality work, design process transparency | Enquiries tagged "brand design" |
| Win social media marketing retainers | Case studies with before/after metrics, pricing tiers | Retainer enquiry submissions |
| Book corporate training & speaking | Credentials page (Scale Up Conference), speaker reel link, topic list | Speaker booking form completions |
| Build owned email list | Newsletter capture across pages, lead magnet (free guide/resource) | Email list growth rate |
| Establish market authority | Blog/Insights with consistent valuable content | Organic search traffic growth, backlinks |

---

## 3. User Goals

### For Gen Z entering real estate:
- Understand if real estate is right for them
- Find a coach who has actually done it (not theory-only)
- Know what the coaching process looks like and costs
- Feel safe enough to make the first contact

### For SME brand clients:
- See proof of design quality quickly
- Understand the difference between Dumbiri and a cheap freelancer
- Book without friction

### For corporate event organizers:
- Verify credentials fast (Scale Up Conference is the anchor)
- See speaking topics and past engagements
- Get a quote or availability

---

## 4. Conversion Goals (by priority)

1. **Book a discovery call** (Calendly embed or form → auto-scheduled)
2. **Submit a service enquiry** (Contact form with service selector)
3. **WhatsApp direct message** (WhatsApp button → pre-filled message)
4. **Email list signup** (Newsletter / lead magnet capture)
5. **Social follow** (LinkedIn, Instagram)

---

## 5. Traffic Goals

| Channel | Goal | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn (existing audience) | Convert profile visitors to website visitors | LinkedIn profile links directly to site; site reinforces LinkedIn content |
| Google organic (SEO) | Rank for Gen Z real estate Lagos, brand designer Nigeria, etc. | Blog content, structured data, meta optimization |
| Referral (word of mouth) | Existing clients share site | Testimonials feature shareable links; portfolio is share-worthy |
| Direct (returning visitors) | Keep clients/students engaged | Blog, email newsletter, fresh content |

---

## 6. Secondary Goals

- **Demonstrate expertise** before a call ever happens (blog, case studies, portfolio)
- **Reduce friction** in the sales conversation by answering objections on the site
- **Pre-qualify leads** through copy that clearly defines who each service is and isn't for
- **Own the narrative** — no longer dependent on LinkedIn's algorithm to reach new people

---

## 7. What Success Looks Like (90 Days Post-Launch)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Monthly unique visitors | 500+ |
| Contact/enquiry form submissions | 20+ per month |
| Discovery call bookings | 10+ per month |
| Email list size | 150+ subscribers |
| Bounce rate | Below 55% |
| Average session duration | 2.5+ minutes |
| Pages per session | 3+ |
| Google search impressions (real estate Lagos, etc.) | Growing month-on-month |

---

## 8. Non-Goals (What This Site Is NOT)

- ❌ Not a personal blog (insights serve lead generation, not journaling)
- ❌ Not an online store (all transactions happen offline or via WhatsApp/call)
- ❌ Not a job board or hiring page
- ❌ Not a replacement for LinkedIn — it's the destination LinkedIn sends people to
